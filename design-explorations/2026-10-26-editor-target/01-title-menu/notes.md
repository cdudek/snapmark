# Title-bar destination

The session becomes the document’s identity: “Ledgerly Q4 review · 6 screenshots ⌄” stays centred in the title bar and opens a native macOS menu of recent sessions, Older Sessions…, New Session ⌃⇧N, and One-Shot: Beautify, then Copy. Jakob’s Law makes this familiar from Mac document windows; Hick’s Law keeps the occasional destination choice compact. Common Region keeps the side column for notes, while Proximity gives each heading more space above than below and a hairline separates the general comment from references. The Von Restorff Effect reserves the single filled blue treatment for Add to Session. The direction follows KEY-1 for New Session, WIN-5 for the editor layout and bottom-right actions, NTE-1 for General comment, NTE-4 for reference spacing, and EDT-8 for keeping coral marks distinct from interface chrome.

- No destination control in the toolbar, notes column, or commit button.
- No separate Session | One-Shot segmented control; One-Shot is a destination in the title menu.
- No custom dropdown cards, extra session-management buttons, or second blue filled action.
- No redesign of the Ledgerly screenshot or editor tools; its Create order button is neutral to preserve the single-blue-element constraint.
