# Branded

Dark theme. The beacon becomes a functional visual language: rounded gradient viewfinder corners frame the captured screenshot and selected mark, cool gradients identify focus and primary actions, and pink diamonds connect numbered marks to their notes. Graphite surfaces echo the app icon while SF Pro, native macOS traffic lights and quiet separators keep the interface familiar. The bright captured page remains the hero across the editor and the session document; the shortcuts window carries the same framing, typography and keycap treatment.

Palette: graphite #0D0D10, #141418, #202024, #2C2C31; hairlines #3B3B42; primary text #F4F4F7; secondary text #A7A7B3; focus/action gradient #20F4EC → #2492FF → #8B3DFF; note/brand gradient #FA6CFF → #EF28A7; remove #EF4444; approve #22C55E; card #FEF9C3; highlighter #FACC15. The captured Ledgerly page retains its own light palette.

Deliberately did not:

- Add new product features, navigation or marketing copy.
- Turn the captured website into a branded or dark-mode surface.
- Use red for notes or primary actions, or green for brand decoration.
- Spread gradients across large surfaces or add neon glow.
- Replace native typography or macOS window controls.
