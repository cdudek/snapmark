# Native

Light theme. Snapmark feels at home on macOS through translucent toolbar and sidebar materials, SF Pro typography, native traffic lights, quiet separators, compact controls and soft window shadows. The captured Ledgerly screenshot remains the editor's focal point. A single beacon blue accents the active tool, primary action and annotations; rounded diamond reference markers and a small beacon in the title area provide the signature details. The document keeps a continuous reading column with three inline regional captures and their numbered notes, while the shortcuts window gives the existing tools and keys an aligned native table treatment.

Palette: accent and annotation blue #2492FF; canvas #F3F3F5; panels #FFFFFF; translucent material base #E9EAEC; primary text #1D1D1F; secondary text #76767C; separators #D8D9DD; remove red #FF3B30; approve green #34C759; text-card yellow #FEF9C3. The small beacon retains cyan #20F4EC, blue #2492FF, violet #8B3DFF, pink #FA6CFF and #EF28A7 on its dark #2C2C31–#0D0D10 tile. Captured webpage colours remain distinct from Snapmark chrome.

- No large brand gradients, glow effects or decorative viewfinder framing.
- No new product flows, navigation or marketing copy.
- No red primary actions or green brand accents; those colours retain their semantic roles.
- No custom display type or oversized chrome competing with the screenshot.
