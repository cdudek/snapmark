DESIGN SYSTEM (binding; the owner's design-system-starter, Snapmark overlay):
- Surfaces: system-like and calm. Window ground #e0e0e0-ish greys only behind content; panels #ededed / #f3f3f3; lifted cards and fields #ffffff. Hairline edges #bebebe, used sparingly. No outlines around controls.
- Text: ink #161616, dim #585858, faint #626262, off/disabled #9e9e9e. Font Inter (or SF Pro): controls 13px/16 regular, captions 12px/16 medium, titles 20px/28 semibold. Only the primary button label is semibold.
- Accent (Snapmark brand blue) #0f72db with white text: fills ONLY the one primary button per region (and an on checkbox/switch). No blue icons, links, tabs, outlines or selected states.
- Selected tab / chosen segment / toggle on: a soft grey pill (black at 7% alpha), ink text. Hover: black at 4%.
- Secondary button: soft grey fill, no border. Ghost: no fill. Icon buttons are ghosts. All controls 32px high, radius 8. Cards radius 12.
- Meaning colours (words and small marks only, never button fills): keep/approve green #00846b on wash #d3f4ea; pass/request-changes magenta #b13290 on wash #ffddf7; warn amber #8e6900 on #f6ebca; error red #c81e48.
- Annotation marks on the page: numbered reference markers in a diamond (like the Mac app), red for remove marks, user colour otherwise.
- Keys appear in tooltips; only the primary may show its key, e.g. "Submit Review ⌘↩".
- HARD RULE: in every row of buttons the primary is the LAST button, at the RIGHT. More (…) goes FIRST in the row. "Request Changes" and "Approve" are no exception: the chosen verdict's submit button is the primary at the right.
