# Snapmark: the capture-to-editor workflow

**The axis:** risk. Safe stays closest to macOS's own ⇧⌘5 and today's Snapmark; Bold pushes one idea all the way; Contrarian questions the shape of the flow itself. Pick one direction; the next round refines it.

**What is being designed** (owner, 2026-10-26), for one person who reviews builds and hands feedback to an AI:

1. **The capture bar (⌃⇧5).** Capture is the one blue button, with its ↩ key shown on it. The chosen mode is a grey fill with a blue icon, so only one thing is blue. A new mode, **Record** (an animated GIF for button animations and loading issues), with its frame rate under Options: 15, 10 (default), 5, 4, 2 fps. **Session vs One-Shot** is visible, not hidden in Options.
2. **The Timer's countdown:** a large 3, 2, 1 on the screen before the capture.
3. **The control while Capture Scrolling or Record runs:** how long or how tall so far, Done ↩, Esc cancels. Record stops at 30 s.
4. **The editor window:** Session | One-Shot as tabs in the side column; choose a new or an older session as the target from the editor; One-Shot shows Beautify (backdrop with a hex field for the custom colour) and Copy closes the window, as Add to session does; the notes column gets more room around "General comment" and "References" and a light divider between them.

**Fixed:** macOS-native look, light and dark; the app's blue #2492FF (filled buttons #0F72DB) for app UI only; annotation marks in the picked colour (coral red #E5484D by default); everything doable with the keyboard.
