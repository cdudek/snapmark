# Safe — Familiar by default

Snapmark follows the macOS Screenshot bar and keeps today’s editor structure: tools across the top, screenshot on the left, and a purpose-built column on the right. Five numbered capture modes sit beside a visible Session / One-Shot choice and session target; conventional Options groups hold recording and timer settings. A large countdown and a compact recording control make timing clear. Session mode gives the general comment and reference notes measured breathing room, a light divider, and an accessible session picker. One-Shot replaces notes with familiar Beautify controls and a clear Copy action that closes the window. Neutral system materials carry the interface, blue identifies app actions, and coral red belongs to annotations.

- No new capture interaction or departure from the horizontal macOS bar.
- No editor layout overhaul, floating tool palette, or hidden session target.
- No blue annotation marks or competing blue filled controls.
- No separate export step after Copy, extra confirmation, or decorative onboarding.
