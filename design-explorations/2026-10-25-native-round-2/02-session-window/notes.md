# Session window

Keep the approved Native appearance: a light macOS window, SF Pro, quiet greys, and an 820px document column.

Two colour systems: app UI uses the logo’s cool brand palette (#20F4EC cyan → #2492FF blue → #8B3DFF violet), with brand blue #2492FF for selected segments, primary buttons (Reopen and Edit Again, with white text), the hover pencil, thumbnail outline, and rename focus ring and text selection; everything else stays neutral grey. Annotations use the user-selected colour, default coral red #E5484D, for screenshot marks, numbered diamonds, and matching document note numbers. Approve ticks always stay green #30A46C. Never mix the two systems.

Keep the session title and “· 12 screenshots” visible in every header, after the traffic lights and app icon. Place Document, Screenshots, and Discarded 3 before Open in Markdown App on the right.

Rename in place by clicking the header title, with a subtle brand-blue pencil on hover. The rename state selects “Ledgerly Q4 review” in a focused field with a brand-blue focus ring and selection and the hint “↩ to save · Esc to cancel”.

Show annotated screenshots in a four-column grid of twelve thumbnails, each with its number, time, and note count. Hover reveals Edit Again and a … menu containing Remove from Session; clicking a thumbnail opens the large, one-at-a-time view with navigation and editing controls.

Discarded 3 lists three screenshots from the last seven days, with thumbnails, discard times, Closed without saving or Removed from session, and Reopen buttons. State the retention period quietly: “Discarded screenshots are kept for 7 days.”
