# Editor

Kept the approved light native macOS editor and Ledgerly content, coral feedback marks, diamond markers, reference badges and toolbar swatch, the green approve tick, and “Ledgerly Q4 review · 12 screenshots” with a chevron. The colour-palette mockup opens the two-row colour palette with coral selected and a rainbow-ring Custom… option.

Two colour systems: app UI uses the logo’s cool brand palette—brand blue #2492FF for primary actions, active tool icons, focus rings, selected states and links, with a light blue tint for the active tool and neutral greys elsewhere. Annotations use the user’s selected colour, default coral red #E5484D, including every screenshot mark and the matching numbered diamonds in References; the toolbar swatch shows that colour, and the approve tick stays green. The in-use and colour-palette mockups use a solid blue “Add to session ⌘↩” button with white text; editor--in-use-gradient.png uses the left-to-right logo gradient #20F4EC → #2492FF → #8B3DFF on that button, with everything else matching the solid in-use variant.
