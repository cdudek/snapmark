# Snapmark: marketing brief

Sell the life after, not the spec sheet. Apple never sold "40x read, 4x write". It sold a thousand songs in your pocket. Every line we put in front of a buyer should describe how their day feels with Snapmark. Features appear only as the quiet reason to believe it.

## The promise

**Your AI finally sees what you see.**

## Elevator pitch

You know what is wrong the moment you see it. Snapmark lets you say it just as fast. Point at the screen, circle what bothers you, and your AI coding partner gets it exactly as you meant it, the first time. Less explaining, fewer rounds, more of the thing you set out to build.

## The story we tell

It is late, and the agent has built the screen you asked for. You can see ten things that are almost right. Today you would type paragraphs: "the second button in the header, the one next to the logo, a bit lower". Then you would wait, and look, and explain again.

With Snapmark you look, point, and keep moving. Each thing you notice takes a second to capture and a word to explain. When you are done, the whole review is in one place, and your agent works through it while you get on with your evening. The next morning it is fixed the way you pictured it.

That is the feeling to sell: **you stay in the flow of judging and creating, and the translating is done for you.**

## Who it is for

People who build software with an AI partner on a Mac: developers, designers and product people who work with Claude Code, Cursor or Codex every day. They have taste and a clear picture of what good looks like. They are fast and keyboard-first, and they hate wasting time. Their frustration is the gap between seeing a problem and getting the AI to understand it. Words are slow and imprecise for things that live on a screen, so they end up explaining the same thing three times.

They already pay for an AI tool, because it gives them leverage. Snapmark is how they get more of that leverage out of every review.

## What they hope for (jobs to be done)

- **To be understood the first time.** "When I see something wrong, I want my AI to get it from one look, so I don't have to explain it twice."
- **To keep their momentum.** "When I review a build, I want to capture everything as I go, without breaking my train of thought."
- **To trust the result.** "When I hand over a review, I want to know the agent is working from exactly what I saw, so the fix matches what I pictured."
- **To look good to others.** "When a teammate or client needs my feedback, I want to send something clear and polished in a click."
- **To feel in control of their work.** "My screenshots and notes stay on my Mac, and nothing private slips out."

## Three things to say, and the proof behind each

Lead with the outcome. The proof is for the curious and goes second, in smaller type.

1. **Show, don't explain.** Your AI sees exactly what you mean.
   - Proof: numbered marks tie every note to a spot on the image; the agent reads the text in the screenshot too.
2. **Review at the speed of thought.** One shortcut, one gesture, next.
   - Proof: capture anything on screen, in any app, with keys that feel like macOS; everything collects in one session as you go.
3. **Yours, and only yours.** Your work never leaves your Mac unless you send it.
   - Proof: plain files on your disk, no account, no cloud; emails, keys and card numbers are blurred automatically.

## Taglines to test

- Point. Done.
- Say it with a screenshot.

## Voice

- Talk about the person and their day, then about the app. "You" more than "Snapmark".
- Short sentences. Calm confidence. No hype words ("revolutionary", "supercharge", "seamless").
- Show the moment: a late review, a client waiting, the fix landing the way you pictured it.
- Name a feature only when it answers "how?". Never lead with it, never list specs (pixel sizes, megapixels, token counts) in headlines.
- Red and green have a meaning in the product: red means remove, green means keep. Use them that way in visuals.

## The offer (planned, not live yet)

USD 12.99, once. Yours to keep. Try it free for 7 days. Launch codes for early fans. Copy should frame the price against the time it gives back ("less than one hour of explaining"), never against other apps.

## Reasons to believe, for the website's lower half

For people who want to know how it works. Keep this below the fold.

- Captures any region, window or scrolling page, anywhere on the Mac.
- Marks: boxes, arrows, pen, remove and keep, numbered references with notes, speech-bubble cards, spotlight, redact.
- One Markdown file per session, readable by any AI agent; PDF or ZIP for people.
- Reads the text in each screenshot; blurs sensitive data before anything leaves.
- One-shot mode makes a single beautiful image for slides and chats.
