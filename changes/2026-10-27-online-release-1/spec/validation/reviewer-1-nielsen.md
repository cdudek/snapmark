# Reviewer 1: Nielsen heuristics

**Method.** I read brief.md, ia.md (objects, alias table, CTA inventory, state diagrams), interaction.md (wireflows, every state table, action behaviour, feedback, errors, MCP activity, WEB rules), every screen in screens.md, owner-decisions.md and intent.md. I grep-checked sitemap.md, microcopy.md and conformance.md for the strings and states the findings cite. I walked each flow first as a new user and then as a returning one: sign-in, first run, capture to feedback in the extension, deciding with versions and a decision request, the copilot implementing and a person accepting, answering and approving on the phone, inviting people, the operator inviting from the waitlist, and linking a Mac session with its sync status. Severity uses Nielsen's 0 to 4 scale, with 4 = Critical and 3 = High in the kit's scale. I raised money, consent, access and mail actions one level, as the B2B rule says. I left out anything already in owner-decisions.md unless its recommended default is itself a 3 or 4. None is, but #32 (single keys on by default) makes finding 1 worse.

**Counts.** Severity 4: 0 · Severity 3: 9 (3 of them contradictions) · Severity 2: 12 (4 of them contradictions) · Severity 1: 6 (2 of them contradictions). Total 27.
