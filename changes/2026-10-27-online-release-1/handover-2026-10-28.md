## The new model (owner, 2026-10-28, A41 to A53)

The owner rejected PR #116's directions (A42: "generally I dont like the direction") and gave a journey instead (A43, A44). The screens are drawn and approved as a direction ("Okay, all very ready", A46): 30 images in `snapmark-web` PR #1, `design-explorations/2026-10-28-feedback-journey/` (gallery, brief, notes with open faults).

- **A comment** is one pin on one element, tied to the commit it was made on, with environment, branch, device and an "uncommitted changes" flag. No title. Optional drawing (the Mac app's tools, A46) and @mention. The pin is a comment bubble with the author's avatar (A41).
- **Several elements (A57):** Shift-click adds or removes an element; a comment then holds one anchor per element, and its screenshot covers all of them (screen 15.2).
- **Element picker** like DevTools, calm: outline, tag, size, component, `file:line`, parent chain, region, module, DOM breadcrumb. It is visible in the feedback (A47, A48).
- **Filters on the page:** Everyone, Only mine, Hide others; Open, Resolved. Older commits behind "Show earlier commits"; a pin whose element is gone is Outdated.
- **Sessions** wrap one person's review pass and can be shared with people or an agent (A52). Comments still belong to their commit.
- **Decisions:** Start a Decision adds Options (mockup upload, Figma link, text). People vote; members decide.
- **Revisions:** the agent reports Revision 1 with its commit; before and after; a reply reopens it for Revision 2; members resolve. Status words: Open, Deciding, In Progress, Revised, Resolved.
- **Move and align (A54):** a Draw | Move switch; drag an element to a new spot, align left, centre or right, spacing and width. The change is a suggestion recorded with the comment as a change list, applied only in the reviewer's browser (screens 23 to 25).
- **Devices** in the extension: Responsive, MacBook Air 13", MacBook Pro 14" and 16", iPad Air, iPhone 16, iPhone 16 Pro Max, Custom (A50). Desktop sizes resize the window; phone sizes need `chrome.debugger` emulation, which shows Chrome's banner.
- **Theme:** Clear Sky, dark by default (A45, A37). The neutral focus ring stays.
- **Logo:** the owner supplies it (A53). The S is a placeholder.
- **Landing page (A58):** release 1 ships a basic placeholder: name, one line, the waitlist form, legal links. The marketing draft in `snapmark-web` `design-explorations/2026-10-28-landing/` was rejected; milestone 6 shrinks to the placeholder and the operator area.
- **Defaults the owner approved with "all very ready":** the eight gaps and four extensions (press `C` to comment, share links, client comment links in release 2, show before on a revised pin), "Options" and "Revisions".
